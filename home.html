Split Dash: Dual Control

Dodge obstacles, split your square, and chase your best score.

Split Dash: Dual Control is a fast neon arcade game built around timing, focus, and quick reactions. Swipe between lanes, avoid obstacles, collect fragments, earn diamonds, and survive as long as you can.

Game Features

  • Fast lane-based arcade gameplay
  • Simple swipe controls
  • Split and merge mechanic
  • Fragment collection system
  • Diamond currency and revive system
  • Near miss skill rewards
  • Score multiplier system
  • Neon visuals, music, and responsive effects
